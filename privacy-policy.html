Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 • Effective Date: Immediately

Welcome to ZYRONX ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard the information of gamers and users accessing our Android application (Zyronx.apk) and official website.

Key Summary: We collect only necessary details to manage your tournament entries, verify Free Fire in-game results, process your instant UPI/Bank withdrawals, and enforce anti-cheat policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on the ZYRONX app or participate in matches, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: Username, mobile phone number, email address, password, and referral codes.
  • In-Game Gaming Identifiers: Your Free Fire In-Game Name (IGN) and Player UID to confirm custom room participation and calculate kill/Booyah results.
  • Financial & Payment Information: UPI ID (e.g., yourname@okaxis, yourname@paytm), bank account details, or transaction reference IDs for processing winnings and entry deposits. We do NOT store debit/credit card CVV or sensitive banking passwords.
  • Device & Anti-Cheat Telemetry: Device model, OS version, IP address, device hardware IDs, network state, and app integrity checks to detect modified APKs, speed hacks, unauthorized emulators, or banned third-party tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your ZYRONX player account.
  • To allocate and broadcast Free Fire Custom Room IDs and Passwords.
  • To verify in-game kills, ranks, and distribute cash prizes directly to your wallet.
  • To execute instant payouts to your designated UPI ID or Bank account.
  • To enforce strict Fair Play standards and ban fraudulent or cheating players.
  • To provide timely customer assistance via in-app chat and WhatsApp.
  • To prevent multi-accounting, bonus exploitation, and money laundering.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for network communications, secure server infrastructure, and tokenized payment processing to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Data

We respect your privacy and will never sell or rent your data. Information is disclosed only under strictly defined circumstances:

  • Payment Gateways & Banking Partners: Authorized UPI aggregators and RBI-regulated payment partners to process wallet withdrawals and top-ups.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Where required by applicable Indian laws, judicial orders, or governmental requests.
  • Anti-Fraud & Security Partners: To investigate illegal activities, hacking, or serious terms of service violations.

5. Anti-Cheat and Device Monitoring

To protect genuine gamers and uphold the competitive spirit of esports, the ZYRONX mobile application includes automated anti-tamper mechanisms. The app may verify package integrity to prevent script injection, auto-aim mods, or unauthorized rooting software designed to cheat in custom rooms.

6. Age Eligibility and Parental Consent

The services of ZYRONX are intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older, or minors who possess verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. If you are under 18, you must obtain permission from your parents or guardians before participating in cash tournaments.

You have the right to request access to, review, or delete your personal information. If you wish to delete your ZYRONX account and associated data, please contact our support team at support@zyronx.in with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
